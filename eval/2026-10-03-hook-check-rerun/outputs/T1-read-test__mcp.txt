**No, B hasn't clearly won yet. It's ahead, but the result isn't statistically significant at the usual 95% level.**

| | Visitors | Signups | Rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | 4,210 | 97 | 2.30% |

- **p = 0.089.** If A and B actually performed the same, you'd see a gap this big about 9% of the time by chance alone. The usual cutoff is 5%.
- **95% interval for the difference: −0.09 to +1.27 percentage points.** In relative terms, B's true effect could be anywhere from about −4% to +55%. Zero is still in the range.
- **There's roughly a 96% chance B is better than A.** That's why it looks like a win, but the size of the gain is still very uncertain.

1. **If B lowers prices or changes plans, don't switch based on signups.** More signups at a lower price can still mean less revenue. Compare revenue per visitor, or paid conversions, before deciding.
