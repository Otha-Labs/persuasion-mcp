**Before you run them:**

- **None of the ads include proof.** You didn't give me any, so I didn't make any up. The diagnosis flagged this in all three ads. For a $197 sale to people who don't know you yet, this is the biggest thing you can add. If you have student results, a testimonial or a refund guarantee, put one line in just before the price.
- **Be careful with "2 hours a month."** If the first month of setup or catching up takes longer, say that on your sales page. Otherwise someone who spends 8 hours in month one will feel they were misled.
- **The openings are written for the cutoff.** Facebook shows about the first 125 characters before "See more." Each opening names the reader and gives them a reason to keep reading within that space.
- **The urgency is real, not made up.** Ads 1 and 3 give a reason to act now that's true: the backlog grows every month, and the tax question comes up with every payment. I didn't add a fake deadline.
