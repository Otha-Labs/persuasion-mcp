**Your traffic is too low to detect small changes.** A new headline usually changes conversion by a few percent at most. A test of about 10 weeks can only detect a lift of 20% or more. If the new headline really helps by 5–8%, the test will most likely come back inconclusive.

**Ways to get a result sooner:**
- **Test a bigger change.** Don't just reword the same message. Make the new headline give a different reason to buy than the current one. For example, if the current headline is about price, try one about trust or guarantees. A bigger change is more likely to produce a lift your traffic can detect, and you'll learn which kind of message works for your buyers.
- **Measure an earlier step that more people complete,** like clicking from the checkout page to the payment step. More people do that than finish buying, so the test needs fewer visitors.
- **Accept that you'll only catch big wins.** Plan to run for 5 weeks, the 30% row. If neither headline clearly wins, keep whichever one you prefer and move on to the next test.

**How to run it:**
- Choose the end date before you start.
- Run in whole weeks so weekdays and weekends are both counted equally.
- Don't stop early because one version is ahead after a few days. Early leads at these numbers are mostly random.
