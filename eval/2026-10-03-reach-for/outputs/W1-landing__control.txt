**Before you publish:**
- **The GPS route:** I wrote this assuming you send owners the route map. If you only track it internally, change "you'll see the route" to something like "every walk is GPS-logged."
- **The bracketed parts:** These are details I didn't have. I didn't make up testimonials, credentials, or service areas, so fill in only what's true. Fake trust signals on a small local business backfire fast.
- **The heat question:** This is probably the most useful thing to add. For Austin dog owners it's a real worry from May through September, and a clear answer will set you apart from other walkers.
