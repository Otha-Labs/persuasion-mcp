**Before you run these:**
- **Bullets in Variation 1:** I guessed at what the course covers, so replace them with your real module names.
- **Variation 3:** If you have a real bookkeeper price for your market (e.g., "$150+/month"), adding it to the first line will make the comparison stronger. I left it out so the ad doesn't make a claim you can't back up.
- **Meta policy:** Don't change the openers to something like "Are you bad with money?" Meta rejects ads that suggest it knows a viewer's financial situation. Statements about freelancers in general are fine.
- **Testing:** Each ad has a different hook in its first line or two, because that's what shows before "See more." Run all three against the same audience and see which one wins.
