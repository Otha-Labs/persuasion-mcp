### Check these before you run them

- **Fill in the bracket in C.** Variation C only works if you're a freelancer yourself. If you aren't, swap that line for a real student result.
- **The "2 hours a month" claim.** I wrote it as "after the first setup" because setup usually takes longer. Change the wording if your course says something different.
- **Course details in A and B.** The "four habits" and the payment examples (deposits, installments) should match what your modules actually cover.
- **Tax timing.** "Three months left in the tax year" and "quarterly tax payments" assume US or Canadian calendar-year filers. If you target the UK, where the tax year ends April 5, change those lines.
- **Refund guarantee.** If you offer one, add a line like "Full refund within 30 days if it's not for you." It's the most common way to lower the risk of buying at this price.

### Why these choices

I planned the ads with the Persuasion Taxonomy, then checked and revised each draft against it. All three cover what a reader needs to know before buying. C covered the most; A is lightest on how bad the problem is, and B on why to trust the person talking.

- **A** opens by [dismissing a whole category](https://taxonomy.coppica.com/disrupt/category-dismissal-then-redirect) (most bookkeeping advice). It then [boils the job down to a few habits](https://taxonomy.coppica.com/reframe/three-lever-reductive-framework) and gives a plain [reason why](https://taxonomy.coppica.com/prove/reason-why-copy) 2 hours is enough.
- **B** describes [how the problem feels](https://taxonomy.coppica.com/identify/felt-sense-description), [follows the mess to what it costs later](https://taxonomy.coppica.com/agitate/downstream-effect), [paints a specific tax-season moment](https://taxonomy.coppica.com/elevate/enargeia), and [comes back to the opening folder](https://taxonomy.coppica.com/bond/callback) at the end.
- **C** uses a [peer-to-peer voice](https://taxonomy.coppica.com/bond/peer-voice-open) and compares $197 once with paying a bookkeeper every month.

None of the ads use a fake deadline. The urgency is the real end of the tax year. Run all three as separate ads in one ad set and you'll see whether rethinking the problem, the pain of the mess, or the price comparison works best.
